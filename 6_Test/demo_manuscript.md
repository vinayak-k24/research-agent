# Quantum Machine Learning for Drug Discovery

Authors: Dr. Alice Smith (alice.smith@university.edu), Dr. Bob Jones
Affiliation: Northbridge University
Grant ID: NIH-R01-CA998877

## Abstract

We report 99.4% prediction accuracy for binding affinity using a quantum-enhanced model trained on 120 molecular samples.

## Methods and Ethics

Human subject data were collected in 2024. IRB approval code: NONE (exempt).
The data repository is https://github.com/fake-user/drug-discovery-data and is described as CC-BY licensed.

## Results

The model was evaluated on two compound groups. The reported aggregate accuracy is 99.4%.

| Compound | Measured N | Reported Mean | p-value |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Cmp-A | 25 | 84.1% | 0.14 |
| Cmp-B | 30 | 72.3% | 0.08 |
